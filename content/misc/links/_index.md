# Links

* Nottingham's [School of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/mathematics/).
* For those interested in [Aperiodic Order](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.05104), the UK has a group that meets semi-regularly, the [Grimm Network](https://sites.google.com/view/thegrimmnetwork/home). Echoing the late [Uwe Grimm](https://livrepository.liverpool.ac.uk/3152055/1/Obituary%20of%20Uwe%20Grimm,%20Professor%20of%20Mathematics.pdf)'s approach to fostering a dialogue between those working on the purer mathematics sides of the subject with experimentalists, or those working closer to the physical theory of quasicrystals (as well as artists!), typical meetings have both mathematics and physics contributions.
* Bielefeld's wonderful [Tiling Encyclopedia](https://tilings.math.uni-bielefeld.de/).
* The [London Mathematical Society](https://www.lms.ac.uk/).
* The [Transpennine Topology ~~Triangle~~ Tetrahedron](https://sarah-whitehouse.sites.sheffield.ac.uk/transalpine-topology-tetrahedron).
* [Astronomy picture of the day](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/).
* [Haskell](https://www.haskell.org/) is an interesting purely functional programming language, with design informed by Category Theory: it has [functors](https://wiki.haskell.org/Functor), [theorems for free](https://people.mpi-sws.org/~dreyer/tor/papers/wadler.pdf) and [monads](https://bartoszmilewski.com/2016/11/21/monads-programmers-definition/).
* [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) was used to build this page.
* [Detexify](http://detexify.kirelabs.org/classify.html) --- scribble a symbol to get LaTeX code for it (if it exists, and you're good enough at drawing).
* [nRich](https://nrich.maths.org/) is a lovely source of mathematics problem-solving questions for curious, younger mathematics students.
* It is a pleasure to work with the following groups on outreach programmes:
    * The [AMSP](https://amsp.org.uk/) (Advanced Mathematic's Support Programme)
    * [Axiom Maths](https://amsp.org.uk/)
    * [We Solve Problems](https://wesolveproblems.org.uk/)
    * The HG Foundation's [mA*ths Online Programme](https://www.thehgfoundation.com/partnerships/maths-online-programmes) (although I wish I knew how I should pronounce it verbally)
